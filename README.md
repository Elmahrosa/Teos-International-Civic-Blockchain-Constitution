## 📜 Executive Summary

The **International Civic Blockchain Constitution (ICBC)** serves as the foundational governance framework of the TEOS ecosystem. It establishes the policy, governance, and operational principles required to support **Sovereign-Aligned Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**.

Unlike traditional permissionless systems, the ICBC places authority in accountable human institutions and policy frameworks. It ensures that digital systems, automated processes, and AI-assisted operations remain transparent, auditable, and aligned with lawful governance.

The Constitution provides a structured approach to digital trust, institutional accountability, and responsible deployment of civic technology.

## ⚖️ Governance & Legal Position

For guidance on the interpretation of governance terminology used throughout this repository, see [docs/LEGAL_POSITION.md](docs/LEGAL_POSITION.md).



To preserve institutional integrity and regulatory clarity, the ICBC operates under the following strict postures:

### 1. No Implied Government Endorsement
Unless expressly authorized through a formal agreement, decree, contract, or statutory instrument, this Constitution should be understood as a governance framework and policy proposal. It does not claim governmental adoption, endorsement, or legal force.

### 2. Engineering Alignment, Not Legal Certification
Components of the TEOS ecosystem are designed to support policy enforcement and operational accountability. Deployment of these systems does not by itself establish legal compliance with any jurisdiction-specific laws or regulations. Organizations remain responsible for independent legal and compliance review.

### 3. Scope of Authority
The Chain of Authority defined within this framework governs the intended operation of the TEOS ecosystem and its participating implementations. It does not assert jurisdiction, control, or legal authority over external organizations or third-party systems unless established through a formal integration agreement.

### 4. Intellectual and Governance Independence
References to implementation repositories are provided for transparency and auditability. The constitutional framework remains independent of any specific software implementation, repository structure, deployment environment, or technology vendor.

## 💎 Institutional Pillars

To support trustworthy and auditable public infrastructure, the ICBC is founded on five non-negotiable principles:

### 1. Digital Sovereignty
Authority resides in lawful institutions and governance frameworks. No algorithm, AI agent, or decentralized protocol independently originates sovereign authority.

### 2. Policy-Before-Execution
Systems exist to implement authorized policy. Technical execution never creates new authority.

### 3. Forensic Auditability
Governance events should be recorded using tamper-evident mechanisms that support transparency, accountability, and evidentiary review.

### 4. Human-in-the-Loop AI
AI systems function as decision-support tools. High-impact decisions remain subject to authorized human oversight.

### 5. Jurisdictional Interoperability
The framework is designed to support alignment with national regulations, public-sector modernization initiatives, and internationally recognized digital governance standards.

## 🏗️ Chain of Authority

The TEOS governance model follows a structured and non-bypassable flow of authority. Each layer derives its mandate from the layer above it and operates within the constraints established by the Constitution.

1. **International Civic Blockchain Constitution (ICBC)**
   Foundational Governance Framework
2. **TEOS-FORGE**
   Infrastructure Integration and Policy Layer
3. **Executable Governance Layer**
   Runtime Governance and Policy Enforcement Infrastructure
